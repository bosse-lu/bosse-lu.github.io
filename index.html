My job, for all these kids, is to make them uncomfortable. That means when I… I'm not coaching them how they are now. I'm coaching them where I see they need to go, and where they're capable of being, which means they've not been there, which means they're going to be uncomfortable all the time. Their job is to learn to be comfortable being uncomfortable.

Comment ça marche

  1. Ouvre l'activité dans Chrome ou Edge, sur ordinateur ou sur téléphone Android.
  2. Quand le navigateur te le demande, autorise le micro.
  3. Écoute le modèle, enregistre-toi, réécoute-toi, puis vérifie ta prononciation.

Tes enregistrements restent sur ton appareil : rien n'est envoyé ni conservé en ligne.
