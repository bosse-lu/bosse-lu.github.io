The rules · Les règles
  • You have 20 seconds for each question.
  • Put your token on the right answer: drag it onto a box, or tap the box.
  • First row = 4 points: you are sure, you choose one letter.
  • Second row = 2 points: you hesitate, you choose two letters (BC, DE, AB, CD or AE).
  • A wrong answer costs 1 point. No answer: 0.
  • Then read the correction and try to beat your best score!
